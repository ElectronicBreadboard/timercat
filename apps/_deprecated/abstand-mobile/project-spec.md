# Abstand

## Vision

Most people don't need another blocker. They need a gesture. A conscious, deliberate act of stepping back from digital noise, for whatever reason, for however long they choose.

Abstand (German: _distance, gap, space_) is that gesture. You select the apps you want distance from, set a duration, configure your own rules, and commit. The app doesn't judge why or for how long. A 20-minute morning break and a 30-day reset are the same act at different scales.

Not a productivity tool. Not a detox program. A discipline tool for people who want to be more intentional about where their attention goes.

## Philosophy

**The commitment is the point.**
The act of creating an Abstand is itself meaningful. The outcome is secondary.

**You set the rules. Abstand reflects how you held them.**
The user decides what the commitment looks like: allow 2 opens, allow 5 minutes, hard block from the start, or just log and observe. Abstand never imposes a standard. It only reflects how closely you followed the one you set.

**The rating is a gradient, not pass/fail.**
Opening an app zero times is better than once, once is better than twice. The river line reflects this honestly. Even a broken Abstand is recorded. A life lived is not a trophy case.

**No judgment.**
Abstand never tells you that you failed. It shows you what happened.

**Scale-invariant.**
A 15-minute Abstand and a 30-day Abstand are the same act at different scales.

## Brand

**Name: Abstand.** Subtitle: _Intentional Distance._

**Feeling:** warm, editorial, grounded. Serif type. Organic forms. Nothing clinical or gamified.

**Visual metaphor: the river.**
Your day flows like a river. When you hold an Abstand cleanly, the river runs straight and clear. When you drift, it bends gently. When you break it, there is a rapid. But the river always continues. It finds its way.

The timeline home screen is the river. A flowing vertical line moving through your day, shaped by how you actually lived it. This is not decoration. The shape of the line is the data.

## Target User

Self-aware people who are already thinking about their digital habits. They've tried blockers. They found them too aggressive or too gamified. They want something quieter: a ritual, not a rescue.

## MVP Screens

**Home: the river timeline**
A vertical scrollable timeline anchored to now. Past scrolls up, future Abstands are visible below. A flowing river line runs through it, shaped by how you held your commitments. Abstand zones appear as warm organic bands along the river. The line runs straight when you held cleanly, curves when you drifted, shows a rapid when you broke it.

For the prototype this is a clean SVG path. The goal is that someone can read yesterday at a glance without labels.

**Create Abstand**
Three steps, no wizard energy.

1. Choose apps to take distance from
2. Set duration
3. Configure your rules: number of allowed opens, total allowed minutes, or no limit (log only)

One deliberate tap to commit.

**Active Abstand**
Pinned at the top of home while running. Shows time remaining and current state. If a hard limit is reached, a quiet screen appears with the Abstand name, time remaining, and an option to end early. No lectures.

**Collectible**
To be decided. Something grounded and natural that reflects how cleanly you held your commitment.

**Not in MVP**
Recurring Abstands, collectibles, badges, social features, Android, widgets, monetization.
