**Promotional text:**


**Description:**

```text
FocusCat is a focus timer for Mac with a cat companion that stays by your side while you work.

Start a pomodoro, write what you're working on, and let FocusCat keep track of the rest. Tap the cat and it meows back. That's the whole experience.

FOCUS PROFILES

Create profiles that block specific apps during sessions. A profile for deep work. One for studying. Schedule them to activate automatically so your focus environment is ready before you are.

ACTIVITY TRACKING

After each session, see which apps you used and for how long. Browse past sessions and spot patterns in how you actually work — not how you think you work.

FOCUS GOAL

Set a daily focus target if you'd like. A quiet progress bar shows where you stand. No streaks, no penalties.

CAT COMPANION

Choose from different faces and hats for your cat. Shuffle for a surprise. Gentle sounds keep the atmosphere cozy — timer ticks, a chime when you finish, and the occasional meow.

PRIVACY FIRST

Activity tracking stays entirely on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. You choose what gets tracked. FocusCat is open source, so you can verify this yourself.

No accounts. No subscriptions. Free forever.
```
